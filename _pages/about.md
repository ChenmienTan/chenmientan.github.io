I am an engineer at Alibaba Group.
I work on infrastructure of reinforcement learning for large language models [[RL2]](https://github.com/ChenmienTan/RL2) [[GEM, ICLR'26]](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=0Ylu01jcnu).
Prior to that, I worked on the algorithm of reinforcement learning, specifically reward modeling [[CHARM]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.10045) [[LR4GPM, AAMAS'23]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.09027) and multi-armed bandits [[ACML'22]](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v189/tan23a/tan23a.pdf). I also worked on knowledge editing [[MALMEN, ICLR'24]](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=L6L1CJQ2PE).
